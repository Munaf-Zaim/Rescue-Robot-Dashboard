About Project

RESQ-AI is a ground robot that searches disaster and flood areas for survivors. It streams live video from an ESP32-CAM over WiFi, receives sensor readings, battery and GPS position over Bluetooth LE, and is driven from this dashboard.

Survivor clues come from PIR motion, vibration (tapping), sound level and AI human detection on the video feed.
